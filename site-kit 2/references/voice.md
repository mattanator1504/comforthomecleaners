# Voice

How every page and post sounds. The rules are the same on every site; **This site** at the bottom is filled in during the first build. Read with `humour-profile.md`.

## How to write

Write like a knowledgeable person explaining something to one customer across a table: straight about the cost, kind about the worry, and quietly annoyed on their behalf at whoever made it complicated.

- **Answer first.** Then the reasons, then the background.
- **Short sentences.** Mostly under 18 words. One idea each.
- **Specific beats general.** A price, a place, a number, a named thing.
- **"I" or "we"**, matching the business. Talk to "you", one reader.
- **Headings say something.** Not "Our Process" but "From first call to finished job". Information first; a joke can follow it.
- **Calls to action say what happens:** "Call now, we answer 7am-7pm", "Get a quote within 2 hours".

## Never use

unlock, leverage, seamless, cutting-edge, world-class, game-changer, elevate, delve, robust, comprehensive, holistic, journey, landscape, "In today's fast-paced world", "We pride ourselves on", "reach out", "affordable", "quality service", "results-driven", "one-stop shop". No exclamation marks in body copy. No emojis.

## AI-writing tells (rewrite if you spot one)

Three "-ing" phrases in a row. "Whether you're an X or a Y". "It's not just X, it's Y". Every paragraph the same length. A closing line that repeats the opening. Claims with no specifics. No personality at all.
