# Supporting pages

**Hub pages** (`/services`, `/industries`, `/areas`, `/use-cases`, `/case-studies`, `/blog`): H1 naming the category, a 2-3 sentence intro, then one real section per child page (name, 2-4 sentences, image, link), then a call to action. Never a bare list of links. In a quick build, the services hub carries a proper section per service even before the service pages exist (no links to pages that don't exist yet).

**About:** the real story, real photos of the people, what they do and don't do, experience and credentials (real only), contact links. `Person` schema for the owner or key people.

**Contact / Quote:** phone (clickable), email, hours, address if real and public, the form (5 fields or fewer), response time. Map only as a static image or on click. Calendar link loads on click.

**Reviews** (only with real reviews): the reviews word for word, with name (as allowed), date and source, linking to the platform. No review schema for your own business.

**Thank-you:** a confirmation in the site's voice, what happens next and when, the phone number, the conversion event firing on load. `noindex`, not in the sitemap or nav.

**Privacy / Terms:** a plain template covering the forms and analytics actually used. Footer links only. Flag them for the owner to check.

**404:** one friendly line, links to home, the main service or product page, and contact.

**Footer (every page):** business name, contact details, main page links, legal links, social profiles. No blocks of city names.
