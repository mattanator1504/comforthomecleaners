# Service page (main money page)

A main service or product page: `/services/[service]`, `/product`, `/features`. These are the pages the business makes money from. They rank for the core commercial keyword ("[service] [city]", "[service] company", "[product category]") and convert.

**Before writing:** search the keyword, read the top 5: their sections, length, prices, FAQs, trust elements, and what they're missing.

## Layout

1. **H1** with the keyword. One-line promise. Main action. One proof point.
2. **What it is:** a 40-60 word answer that stands on its own.
3. **Who it's for**, and one honest line on who it isn't for.
4. **What's included** or key features: 5 bullets max.
5. **Price** or what changes the price (real numbers only; otherwise "price confirmed before any work starts").
6. **Proof:** reviews mentioning this service, a project, a case study, real photos.
7. **How it works:** 3-4 steps.
8. **Areas or industries:** links to this service's SEO pages (city, industry, use case), once they exist.
9. **FAQ:** 3-5 real questions, straight answers.
10. **Call to action.**

## Rules

- Sales copy, not a document. Keep the visible page tight; put longer detail in collapsible `<details>` sections (Google still reads them).
- Screenshot test: with everything collapsed, does the page still sell?
- Each secondary keyword gets a section or a natural home.
- Humour: light. Never in the price, the FAQ answers or the call to action.
- Linked from the services hub, the homepage and the nav.
- `Service` schema (or `Product` / `SoftwareApplication`), `BreadcrumbList`, `FAQPage`.
