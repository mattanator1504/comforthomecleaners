# Home page

**Job:** in five seconds, say what this is, who it's for and where; prove it; get the visitor to the main action or to the right page.

Match the section order to what the top 5 results do, then beat them. A typical order:

1. **Hero:** H1 that says plainly what they do and for whom (and where, if local). One line of promise. The main action, plus phone for local businesses. One proof point (a real number, rating or client).
2. **What they offer:** a short block per service or product, each linking to its page.
3. **Proof:** real reviews, results, projects, client logos, photos of real work.
4. **How it works:** 3-4 steps from first contact to done.
5. **Why them:** the specific differences, not adjectives.
6. **Areas or industries** (if the site has those pages): a short section linking to each.
7. **FAQ:** 3-5, only if the top results have one.
8. **Final call to action.**

- About 400-800 words, matched to the top results
- Title: `Brand: [what they do] in [City]` or `Brand | [what they do] for [who]`
- Humour: light (see `references/humour-profile.md`)
- Links to every main page; the homepage is the strongest page on the site, so it points to the pages that matter most
