# Blog post

Answers one real question people search, keeps them reading (that's what the humour is for), and sends them to the money page.

**Pick the topic:** a question buyers search before hiring or buying ("how much does [service] cost", "[thing] vs [thing]", "how to know if you need [service]"). People Also Ask is a good source. Skip anything already in `keywords-used.md`.

**Before writing:** read the top 3-5 for the question: length, H2s, tables, FAQs, what they miss. Find original sources for any statistics and check each number is really there.

## Layout

1. **H1:** the question, the way people search it.
2. **Opening hook:** 1-3 funny, specific sentences (see `references/humour-profile.md`).
3. **Quick answer:** 40-60 words, straight, including the key number.
4. **Why this site knows:** one line of real experience (skip if there isn't any; never fake it).
5. **A table** near the top: prices, a comparison or a checklist.
6. **Body:** one H2 per real question, answer first in each, then the detail and the jokes.
7. **What we actually see:** first-hand detail nobody else has (only if real).
8. **FAQ:** 4-6, straight answers.
9. **Ending:** a line that lands, a clear link to the money page, one "read next" link.
10. **Author box** (company and service sites: the real author; rank and rent: the brand).

## Rules

- **Length:** around the average of the top 3 results.
- **Write it straight first, then do the humour pass** bottom-up at full blog density (see `references/humour-profile.md`). Quick answer, FAQ, table, numbers and the call-to-action sentence stay straight.
- Two in-text links to the money page (about a third and two-thirds down). Every statistic linked to its original source.
- `BlogPosting` schema with author, `FAQPage`, `BreadcrumbList`. Published and updated dates visible.
- Add it to the blog index; add its jokes to "Used bits" in `references/humour-profile.md`.
