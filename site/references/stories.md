# Stories

Real stories from the business. Pages and posts use these when an anecdote helps. **Never make one up, and never add details to one.** No story fits? Write without one.

- One story per post at most
- Keep the bones (the situation, the one thing that was fixed, one specific detail), adapt the telling
- Client names only if they're already public or the owner agrees
