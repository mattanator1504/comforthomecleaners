# Opinions

The owner's real, specific opinions about their work and industry. They make the writing sound like a real person and give pages something no competitor has. **Only opinions the owner would say out loud; never invent or sharpen one.**

- One strong opinion per post at most
- Back it with a number or a story
- About behaviours, never named competitors
- The most persuasive kind: when NOT to buy from them

**Good questions to draw them out:** What do customers always ask for that you talk them out of? What's the most common mistake you see? What does everyone in your industry do that you think is wrong? What would you tell a friend before they hired anyone in your trade?
